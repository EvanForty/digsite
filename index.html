FIELD NOTES

A LITTLE
DIGGING DEEPER.

Do I need to buy tokens to play?+

No. The core game is designed to be free to play. Creator fees fund rewards; buying tokens or paying for a dig is not required. Exploring the quarry needs no wallet.

Are these real Pokémon cards?+

DIGSITE features authentic Pokémon card artwork and an in-game collection. Physical Pokémon cards and graded slabs are part of the rewards roadmap. Physical rewards are not currently available to claim.

How will I receive a treasure I find?+

At launch, a verified discovery would be recorded for the winning player. The planned Collector Crypt integration would support redemption of the collectible. Wallet verification, inventory custody, and shipping are not yet available.

What can I do in the quarry?+

Run and jump through a 3D quarry, meet Pikachu, Eevee, Diglett, and Dugtrio, collect gold, and discover distant landmarks. Talk to Pokémon for clues or invite one to follow you. Excavate three treasures to unlock the Golden Chamber. Your progress stays in this browser.
